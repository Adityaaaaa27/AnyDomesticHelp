Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 7, 2026

Welcome to AnyDomesticHelp ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of the personal information you share with us through our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the AnyDomesticHelp app, we may collect the following personal information:

  • Personal Contact Information: Name, phone number, email address, and city/address provided during employer registration, referral forms, or feedback submissions.
  • Employment & Referral Details: Job category preferences, experience details, and reference information submitted when referring helpers or registering as a partner.
  • Device & Notification Data: Device push notification tokens to send updates regarding service inquiries and status notifications.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To match domestic help service requirements with verified candidates in Mumbai.
  • To contact employers, partners, and referrers regarding their service requests.
  • To improve our mobile application features, customer service, and user experience.
  • To send essential push notifications and status updates about your service requests.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We prioritize your privacy and do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third-party advertisers. Information is only accessed by authorized AnyDomesticHelp staff to fulfill your domestic service requests.

4. Data Security

All data transmitted between the AnyDomesticHelp mobile application and our servers is encrypted in transit using standard HTTPS protocols. We maintain administrative and technical safeguards to protect your personal data against unauthorized access.

5. Your Data Rights & Deletion

You have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your personal information stored with AnyDomesticHelp. To submit a data deletion request, please email us at support@anydomestichelp.com with your registered phone number or email address.
